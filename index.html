      Client app + front desk console Built and working, not yet in use

      A veterinary clinic in Ontario

      A client app and a front desk console, built to sit alongside the clinic's existing practice software rather than replace it.

      • Clients request food and medication refills from the pet's file, and follow each request through to ready for pickup, without phoning.
      • The app tells clients before food or a prescription runs out.
      • It never calculates a vaccination due date. It only repeats what staff entered, so it cannot tell someone their dog is due when it is not.

      One refill request, start to finish

      1. Requested in the appclient
      2. Being looked atdesk
      3. Approvedvet
      4. Ready for pickupdesk
      5. Collected, points awarded in one tapcounter

      The client sees every step. No phone call at any of them.

      Workout app In use

      A personal trainer

      The trainer builds programs and assigns them, clients log their sets on their phone, and both see progress over time.

      • Built on the idea that clients quit when they cannot see it working, so the product is the evidence of progress, not the logging.
      • Each client sees only their own data. That rule is enforced by the database, not just hidden on the screen.

      A set logged in a basement gym

      1. Logged on the phone, no signalclient
      2. Saved on the phone firstoffline
      3. Signal back, sent automaticallysync
      4. On the trainer's progress chartstrainer

      Nothing is lost when the gym has no reception.
